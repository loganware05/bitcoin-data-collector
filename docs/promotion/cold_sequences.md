# Cold sequences and launch copy

Sellbird-shaped: short, plain text, one idea, no pitch in email 1.  
Approve every Outbox draft. Zero links in email 1. At most one link later (prefer Agensi).

Campaign goal: reply or "want the sample report?" — not a booked demo.

---

## Sequence hygiene checklist

- [ ] Sending domain warmed; daily cap respected
- [ ] Email 1 has zero links and no product pitch
- [ ] Facts about the prospect come from their site / public pages only
- [ ] Opt-out honored immediately
- [ ] No performance guarantees, no "edge you can bank"
- [ ] Every send human-approved until autopilot is deliberately enabled
