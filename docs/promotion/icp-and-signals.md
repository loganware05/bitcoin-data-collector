# ICP and Sellbird signals

Use with Sellbird Discover / cohort builder. Prefer lists of ≤50.

## Must-have (ICP)

A good prospect matches **at least one**:

1. Builds or sells Kalshi / Polymarket / prediction-market products
2. Publishes BTC or crypto market research tooling
3. Ships agent skills, MCP servers, Claude Code / Cursor workflows
4. Hiring quant, data, or ML roles tied to markets research

## Nice-to-have

- Recently funded AI / fintech (raise announced on their own site within ~18 months)
- Accelerator / YC-backed (use Sellbird startup skill timing; never open with congrats)
- Mentions Python market data, probability models, or calibration publicly

## Disqualifiers

- Wants automated order execution / "set and forget" trading
- Asks for guaranteed profits or financial advice
- Pure consumer retail with no builder / research angle
- Agencies pitching SEO/generic AI with no market-data product

## Suggested Sellbird signal confirmations

Confirm signals that help Scout personalize (names vary by Sellbird version):

- Product/service mentions prediction markets, Kalshi, Polymarket, or BTC
- Hiring pages listing quant / data / ML / research engineer
- Tech stack or blog mentioning agents, MCP, Claude, Cursor
- Funding / accelerator mentions (for timing only, not flattery openers)
- Contact is founder, head of research, or technical lead (not generic info@ only)

- `"Kalshi" API OR SDK OR bot site:.com -site:kalshi.com`
- `"Polymarket" market making OR research tooling`
- `"agent skill" MCP bitcoin OR kalshi`
- Job posts: `"quant researcher" bitcoin` OR `"prediction market" engineer`

## Personalization facts worth mining

From the prospect's own site only:

- Exact product name and what it does
- A public market they support
- A role they are hiring
- A research post or docs page about odds / pricing

Never invent mutual connections, fake "Re:" threads, or performance claims.
